## Reimbursements
Employees may claim reimbursement for pre-approved business expenses. Claims must be
submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Receipts are mandatory for any claim
above 500,000 IRR. Claims without a valid receipt above this threshold are rejected.

## Client Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses require prior written approval from the account lead.
The per-client entertainment cap is 20,000,000 IRR per quarter.

## Travel
Domestic travel is reimbursed at actual cost for economy transport and standard lodging.
International travel requires finance-director approval before booking.

## Payment Terms
Standard supplier payment terms are net 45 days from invoice date. Early-payment
discounts, where offered by a supplier, should be taken when cash position allows.
